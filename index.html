GUIDED WALKTHROUGH

What every knob actually does

01

Write bounded text

Sal self-reference in the plural and attribute decisions to the actual system. Arbitrary text stays in development mode.

02

Choose the ensemble

One member sounds exposed. Five create canonical Sal. Continue through fifteen to hear increasing density without duplicating a recording.

03

Shape register and tone

Register shifts perceived pitch. Tone changes low/high spectral balance. Neither is a truthful “gender” switch.

04

Place the voices

Width controls pan, micro-delay creates depth, and small member variation adds shimmer. Always run the mono check.

05

Respect identity limits

Sal v0 inherit traits from a single-speaker public-domain corpus. A less gender-coded timbre requires new consented data and evaluation—not an EQ preset.

06

Export a recipe

Save the audition settings, render source members from Modal, then reproduce the mix offline with the included deterministic recipe tool.
